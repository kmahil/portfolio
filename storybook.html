Guide of the Eternal Soul

For a class project, in a group of four, we created an interactive storybook based on a script from fellow classmates Kristen Lee and Kaleigh Tran. The story was built on Vev, and I focused on creating interactions and adding special effects.

Tools: Clip Studio Paint, Figma, Vev

Skills: special effects, interactions

Role: interaction designer

Script Editing

When reviewing the script, we noticed that the original story had some areas that needed to be further developed. We used the original story as a baseline and rewrote the script for a stronger narrative. The original characters stayed the same, but we added more depth. We also change the plot of the story to add dimension to the narrative.

Moodboard

The story takes place in a fantasy world, therefore we took inspiration from fantasy art. We explored purples, blues and pink to give the story a magical element. Our team illustrator had a specialized style of drawing, so much of the inspiration was later changed to fit her drawing style.
