About Me

Hi there! I’m Keerat, a designer, book lover and occasional runner. Having completed my bachelor's degree in a multidisciplinary design program, I realized that web design and motion design were what I was most passionate. Since then, I have been honing my skills in HTML, CSS, AfterEffects and more. Being a lifelong reader, my approach to design has always been to tell a story. I hope that with everything I create, I can draw people in and let them experience a story they otherwise may not have seen.
