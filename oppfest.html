OppFest Competition Video

Hosted by Simon Fraser University, Opportunity Festival is a competition for students to showcase and pitch innovative and entrepreneurial ideas, while receiving mentorship for their startups. I created a motion graphic video that was shown at the start of the competition.

Tools: Figma, AfterEffects, Illustrator

Skills: video editing, animation

Role: motion designer

Sketches

I started by drawing a storyboard of what the animation could look like. This helped me in getting my ideas across and testing to see if they were feasible. The theme of the competition was 'unlock your potential', and there were 6 keys that the branding was based on. These keys were used frequently throughout the design assets, therefore I decided to base the video around the keys.

Drafts

After creating sketches, I started building the mockup on AfterEffects. During my drafts, I focused on lining up the keys to create cohesion and making sure that the animation flowed smoothly. I also incorporated the competition slogan to further emphasize the meaning of the keys.
